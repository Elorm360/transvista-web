## In one line

**TransVista Africa coordinates Ghana trips end to end.** Travellers share
where they want to go, or just the kind of trip they want. TransVista
coordinates experiences, transport and stays, and sends one clear proposal
within 48 hours.

- **Tagline:** Travel. Explore. Connect.
- **Mission:** Making travel across Africa simple, trusted and accessible.

## The promise

1. **One connected journey.** Transport, experiences, stays and local
   partners in one plan, not a stack of separate bookings.
2. **Vetted partners, clear prices.** Partners are vetted before they join,
   and travellers agree one clear price with TransVista before paying.
3. **Local expertise.** Guides and partners are rooted in the communities
   visited, and they are people travellers can actually reach.
4. **Speed.** A proposal within 48 hours.

## What we offer

| Offer | What it is | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Custom trips | Tailored plans across all Ghana regions: experiences, transfers, accommodation, or a complete itinerary | `/plan` |
| Group trips | Scheduled shared departures with fixed price, itinerary and inclusions | `/trips` |
| Destinations and experiences | Curated places and ways to experience them, which lead into planning | `/destinations`, `/experiences` |
| Community | A waitlist and newsletter for travellers and industry partners | Homepage |

## How it works (the customer journey)

1. **Tell us about your trip:** destination, dates, group, interests, or
   just a starting idea.
2. **We coordinate:** routes, stays, experiences and trusted providers.
3. **Receive your proposal:** availability, a recommended plan and a clear
   price within 48 hours.
4. **Confirm and prepare:** pay TransVista the deposit or full amount, then
   meet the confirmed provider.

## Who it's for

- **Diaspora and international visitors** discovering or returning to Ghana,
  often for heritage (Cape Coast, Elmina) and culture (Kumasi).
- **Ghana residents** looking for well-organised weekend and group trips
  (Volta waterfalls, Central Region day trips). Group trips are where the
  community and networking feel matters most.
- **Industry partners:** tour operators, transport providers, guides,
  accommodation and tourism organisations, who join through the community.

## Voice

- **Warm, calm and specific.** Say "Two nights in the hills is the version
  people recommend to friends", not "Amazing unforgettable adventure!"
- **Honest about effort and history.** For example, Cape Coast "is not a
  photo stop", and Mole "assumes at least two nights".
- **Trust before hype.** Lead with what is confirmed: prices, deadlines,
  what's included.
- **British spelling** (traveller, organised, colour).

## Design principles

From [DESIGN_SYSTEM.md](DESIGN_SYSTEM.md):

1. Trust before beauty.
2. Clarity over complexity.
3. Mobile first.
4. Accessibility by default.
5. Consistency over novelty.
6. Performance is part of the user experience.
7. Authentic African storytelling: original photography, real traveller
   stories.
8. Every interaction should reduce uncertainty.
